What Makes a House Work?

Have you ever wondered why your house stays warm in the winter and cool in the summer? Or why some houses last for hundreds of years while others fall apart in just a few? That's what building science is all about.

A house is not just a pile of wood, bricks, and nails. It's a system — meaning all the parts work together. The roof, the walls, the heater, the windows, and even the dirt around the foundation all affect each other. Change one thing, and the whole house responds.

On this site, you'll explore the seven big ideas that builders, engineers, and scientists use to design homes that are safe, comfortable, healthy, and built to last. Click any topic below to dive in!
